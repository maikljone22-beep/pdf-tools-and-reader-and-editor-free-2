Read-only comparison against `D:\pdf\SmartDocs-project-v0.4.0` (main, at commit 359f563, which includes the nitro-tools merge). Code paths are relative to `app/src/main/java/com/smartdocs/editor/`. Strings: `res/values/` (Arabic) and `res/values-en/` (English). The "Nitro?" column gives the `cmp_xx:line` row where one exists.
