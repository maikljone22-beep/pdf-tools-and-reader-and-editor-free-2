## 3. Verified by running

- **MultiPage (D10).** I compiled `core/fillsign2/MultiPage.kt`, `CombLayout.kt` and `core/share5/ShareRules.kt` with the embeddable kotlinc (harness in `scratchpad\tabsaudit\h4\Main.kt`, no gradle). Output:
  - Rotated A4 (/Rotate 90; displayed 842×595, user space 595×842). Stamp Box(450,700,550,750) relocated to the **same** page gives Box(450,**545**,550,595), so it moves 155 pt. `pasteBox` gives Box(371,272.5,471,322.5), re-centred although the stamp fits.
  - Crop box offset to [100 100 695 942]. Stamp (600,850,680,900) relocated gives (515,792,595,842); `pasteBox` re-centres it.
  - `pages(ODD, except 0)` = [2,4] and the Arabic-digit range "٢-٣" = [1,2] are both correct. The comb RTL placement fills the rightmost cell first (correct), and extra characters are truncated (D29).
- **Strings.** A script (`scratchpad\tabsaudit\strchk.py`) checked every `R.string` / `R.plurals` referenced from the Fill & Sign, Sign, signature, forms5, FormFillSheet, esign, ReaderScreen and NvePanes sources (1022 ids) against `res/values` (ar) and `res/values-en`. None is missing in either language.
- **Not run (needs device or Android PDFBox):**
  - D1, D2, D3 and D5 follow directly from the code: `doc.save(out)` (full rewrite) on a signed document always breaks the byte-range digest.
  - D9, D11 and D20 are layout/rendering findings to confirm on the SM-T970 (Arabic UI, a /Rotate 90 page).
