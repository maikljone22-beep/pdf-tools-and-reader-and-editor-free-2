# Convert audit, group C: non-PDF conversions, PDF to PDF transforms, and the hub itself

Scope: (a) every Conv that is neither to-PDF nor from-PDF (img_convert, heic_jpg, img_resize, img_txt, img_docx, docx_txt, docx_html, docx_md, docx_odt, odt_docx, docx_epub, docx_images, xlsx_csv, csv_xlsx, xlsx_ods, pptx_images, pptx_handouts, pptx_odp, txt_docx, md_docx, md_html, md_epub, html_docx, epub_txt, epub_docx); (b) PDF to PDF (pdf_pdfa, pdf_gray, pdf_compress, pdf_flatten, pdf_searchable, img_ocr_pdf, and pptx_handouts with PDF input). For OCR I checked only crashes, freezes, cancel, memory, options and batch, not recognition quality; (c) the hub: ConvertHubScreen, Cv6OptionsSheet, Cv6Labels, Cv6Results, T5Job/T5JobUi, search, catalog/engine consistency, strings, process death and rotation, and how the hub is reached.
Paths are relative to `app/src/main/java/com/smartdocs/editor/` unless marked otherwise. This audit was read-only and made no project edits.

## Verified by running

Harness: `C:\Users\User\AppData\Local\Temp\claude\D--pdf\fdc86c7c-3184-4367-a941-8255d2dde9f3\scratchpad\convaudit\c\` (src/Main.kt, compiled against the real convert6/office/openall/convert sources with kotlinc 2.3.21 and kxml2; output in run.txt and out/). - **Works:** Arabic DOCX (chemistry, 491 paragraphs and 9 tables) to DOCX/EPUB/ODT/TXT/MD. All XML parts are well-formed, Arabic text is identical to the source (19954 non-space chars in and out, nothing missing), there are 644 `<w:bidi/>` paragraphs, and ODT read-back gives 500 blocks.
