# Convert audit — Group A: everything → PDF

Read-only audit of main `D:\pdf\SmartDocs-project-v0.4.0`. Paths are relative to `app/src/main/java/com/smartdocs/editor/` (the WebView writer is `app/src/main/java/android/print/WebPdfWriter.kt`).

**Scope:** the hub conversions `word_pdf`, `excel_pdf`, `ppt_pdf`, `images_pdf`, `tiff_pdf`, `text_pdf`, `md_pdf`, `html_pdf`, `url_pdf`, `epub_pdf`, `eml_pdf`, `scan_pdf` (route → ScannerScreen), `clipboard_pdf` and `any_pdf`. Also the PDF-output parts of `pptx_handouts` and `img_ocr_pdf`, ToPdfPlusScreen + CombinePlusUi/CombinePlus, ShareToPdfScreen/ShareImport, T5PrintService (the virtual printer) and the old `ui/tools/ConvertTool.kt`.

**How it works:**
- The hub calls `Cv6Engine.run`, then `Cv6Engine2.toPdf`, then `ToPdf6.convert` / `ToPdf6.combine`.
- `ToPdf6` calls the tools5 `ToPdf` for DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, EPUB, EML, HTML and Markdown. Those are printed through `WebPdfWriter` (a headless WebView print). Images go through `ImageIo6.imagesToPdf` (PdfBox).
- ToPdfPlus, ShareToPdf and CombinePlus use **only** tools5 `ToPdf`. That is a different engine from the hub.

## Verified by running

The real sources were compiled with the PC Kotlin compiler (no gradle): `FileKind6`, `TiffDecoder`, `Images6`, `CfbReader`, `LegacyOffice`, `FlowDoc`, `FlowReaders`, office models, `SourceReaders` and `TextNorm`. The harness is at `scratchpad/convaudit/a/` (`build.sh`, `src/Main.kt`, `src/Clip.kt`, inputs in `in/`, outputs `run1.txt`, `run2.txt`). The inputs were copies of two real `.doc` files from Telegram Desktop plus synthetic files made with PIL.

- **TIFF memory (A-H1):** `scan12.tif` (12 pages, A4 at 300 dpi, grey, uncompressed, 100 MB):
  - at `-Xmx512m`, `TiffDecoder.decode(readBytes)` fails with **OutOfMemoryError** after 9 s;
  - at `-Xmx1500m` it succeeds but uses **717 MB**.
- **JPEG-in-TIFF (A-M16):** `jpegtiff.tif` gives `unsupported=7` with no pixels, so the page is dropped.
- **Fake .xls (A-H11):** `statement.xls` (HTML) and `export.xls` (TSV) are both detected as `XLS`. `XlsReader.read` then throws `IllegalArgumentException: not a compound file`, which is the raw text the user would see.
