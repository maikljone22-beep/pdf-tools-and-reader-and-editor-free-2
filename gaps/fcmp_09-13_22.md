Main entry points:

- **Tools tab:** `ui/tools/ToolsScreen.kt:73-76` (search_all, search_highlight, find_replace, compare), routed in `ui/tools/ToolScreens.kt:55,80-82`.
- **Edit5 hub:** Document properties is `e5_doc_props` at `ui/edit5/Edit5Hub.kt:46,75`.

"Nitro?" says whether the feature is in the Nitro list, with the cmp_xx row (`file:line`) where one exists.
