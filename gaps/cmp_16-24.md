# Nitro PDF Pro vs SmartDocs: sections 16–24

Source: `D:\pdf\SmartDocs-project-v0.4.0\app\src\main\java\com\smartdocs\editor` (paths below are relative to it). This was a read-only review: nothing was built or run.

**How things are reached in the UI**
- Tools grid: `ui/tools/ToolsScreen.kt:40-96` lists the cards and `ui/tools/ToolScreens.kt` routes them. The edit5 tools open through the `e5_hub` card (`ToolsScreen.kt:93` → `ToolScreens.kt:100` → `ui/edit5/Edit5Hub.kt:53/82`).
- Reader: side panels are defined in `ui/reader/ReaderToolbars.kt:57`, `enum ReaderPanel {OUTLINE, BOOKMARKS, ANNOTATIONS, THUMBNAILS, INFO}`.
- Settings: `ui/settings/SettingsScreen.kt`, plus `ui/settings/Gaps6SettingsSection.kt` and `ui/cloud/SyncSettings.kt`.

**Status values**
- HAVE: implemented and reachable from the UI.
- PARTIAL: some options are missing.
- CODE-ONLY: the logic exists but nothing in the UI calls it.
- MISSING: searched for and not found.
- N/A-ANDROID: desktop-only, or not feasible on Android.

Importance means importance for a phone or tablet PDF app.
